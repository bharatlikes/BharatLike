Why Instagram Likes, Comments & Reposts Matter

Instagram engagement helps show how people interact with your content. Each engagement type serves a different purpose.

  • Likes: Allow users to express appreciation for a photo, Reel, or other post.
  • Comments: Create opportunities for discussion, feedback, and interaction.
  • Reposts: Help users share content with their own audiences, potentially introducing it to new viewers.

A balanced Instagram strategy focuses on creating valuable content and encouraging genuine interactions rather than relying entirely on engagement numbers.

How Instagram Reposts Can Support Content Visibility
